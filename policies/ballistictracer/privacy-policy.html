Privacy Policy - Ballistic Tracer

Last Updated: October 16, 2026 (app version 3.0.1)

한국어 버전 (Korean)

This Privacy Policy describes how hashbrown ("we", "developer") handles personal information and device permissions in the golf shot tracer application Ballistic Tracer.

1. Information We Collect

Ballistic Tracer does not require user registration or logins. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

Ballistic Tracer does not request camera, microphone, or photo/video (storage) read permissions. The features and permissions it uses are listed below; all data is processed on the device and never transmitted.

  • Importing a video: Uses the Android system file picker. The app can access only the single video you choose, not your whole gallery.
  • Recording: Opens your phone's built-in camera app. The camera app records the video, and Ballistic Tracer receives only the finished clip.
  • Impact sound detection: Does not use the microphone. The app analyzes the sound track inside the video file you chose, on your device.
  • Saving results: Adds new videos to the Android media store (Movies/탄도라인 folder). No permission is required for this.
  • Foreground service and notifications (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK): Keep video analysis and export running when you leave the app or the screen turns off, and show progress and completion notifications. If you deny notifications, the rest of the app still works.

3. 100% On-Device Processing

Imported videos, their sound, extracted trajectory data, saved projects (My Work list), and exported videos are processed and stored only on your device. We do not operate external processing servers or cloud storage and cannot access your data. Saved projects are deleted when you remove them from the My Work list or uninstall the app.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Ballistic Tracer contains zero advertisements (No AdMob) and zero third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. No data is shared with third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

Ballistic Tracer is a sports utility application suitable for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
