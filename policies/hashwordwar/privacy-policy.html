Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026 · App: 영어대난투: 해시 워드 워 (HASH WORD WAR) (org.hashbrown.hashwordwar)

🛡️ Safety Summary: "영어대난투: 해시 워드 워 (HASH WORD WAR)" is a 100% free educational action game designed for students and families. It does not collect any personal data, contains no ads, has no in-app purchases, and requires no account registration.

1. Personal Information Collection

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, device IDs, or physical location.

  • No account registration or login required.
  • Contains no third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

2. Local Device Storage

Game progress (cleared stages, collected stars, and player preferences such as volume and controls) is saved strictly on the user's local device.

  • No gameplay or progress data is uploaded to remote servers.
  • Uninstalling the application will delete all local save data.

3. Application Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Exclusively used for local peer-to-peer Wi-Fi multiplayer battles. No personal data is transmitted over the internet to remote servers.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide haptic feedback when words are loaded and fired.

4. Children's Online Privacy & Families Policy

This application is family-friendly and fully complies with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

  • We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.
  • No third-party advertisements or external links that compromise student safety.
